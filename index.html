The Studio

01 / 03

We design buildings that hold their silence — spaces that let light, material, and daily life do the talking.

Founded on the belief that architecture should be felt before it is noticed, Tiba works at the intersection of structure and restraint. Every project begins with a question about how people actually live, and ends with a building that answers it slowly, over years.
